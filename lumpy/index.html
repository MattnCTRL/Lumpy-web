FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is Lumpy? +

Lumpy is an orchestration platform for AI engineering. It runs and supervises fleets of Claude Code agents across your projects and servers, monitors the health of everything, and ships changes with self-healing, auto-rollback deploys — all coordinated by an always-on Conductor.

Do I need to babysit the agents? +

No. That's the point. Agents run autonomously on the tasks you give them, and the Conductor keeps the fleet healthy and productive. You step in only when you want to — and you can do that from your phone.

How do self-healing deploys work? +

Changes go through build, type-check, and health verification before they go live. If anything fails or the platform doesn't come back healthy, Lumpy automatically rolls back to the last known-good version — so a bad change never takes you down.

What can it monitor? +

Servers, machines, and remotes (CPU, memory, disk, load), plus hosted-service uptime checks. When something crosses a threshold or goes offline, Lumpy raises an alert and the Conductor triages it.
